# Rubric: is this a good first issue?

## Checks

| Check               | Evidence                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Pass condition                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Weight    |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------- |
| maintainer-responds | The "maintainer first-response sample" list and the "last 5 default-branch commits" list under Repo facts, plus the author association (OWNER, MEMBER, COLLABORATOR) shown on the issue opener and on each comment in the Comments section | At least 1 of the 5 sampled issues received its first maintainer comment within 30 days, OR the issue itself was opened by, or has a comment from, an OWNER, MEMBER, or COLLABORATOR, OR at least 1 of the last 5 default-branch commits is by a human author (name not ending in [bot]) within 30 days of the capture date                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | required  |
| repo-active         | The "archived:" flag on the repo line and the "last push to any branch" date under Repo facts                                                                                                                                              | archived is "no" AND the last push is within 90 days of the bundle's capture date (in live mode, within 90 days of today)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | required  |
| not-claimed         | The "this issue: assignees:" and "linked PRs:" line under Repo facts, plus the Comments section read for claim comments ("I'll take this", "working on this", "/assign")                                                                   | No assignee is set, no linked PR is in the open state, and no claim comment was posted within the last 60 days of the capture date unless a maintainer later replied releasing the issue. A PR mentioned only in the comments counts the same as a linked PR                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | required  |
| scope-fits | The issue header (the opener's author association and the labels line), the issue body, the full comment thread, and the "linked PRs:" line under Repo facts | The issue describes one coherent piece of work with a clear end state, even if it touches several files. Fail only if one of these is true: the issue is explicitly an umbrella or tracking issue whose sub-items are meant to become separate PRs; it is a usage or support question; the thread shows the design is still being debated with no maintainer decision; a maintainer states the fix touches core internals; the issue is a new-feature request that no maintainer has acknowledged (not opened by an OWNER, MEMBER, or COLLABORATOR, no comment from one, and no label applied), because silence on a feature is not a settled design; the issue was opened by a bot account (opener name ending in [bot]); or the issue has 2 or more closed, unmerged linked PRs, because repeated abandoned attempts mean the work is harder than the label says. A terse body still passes if the work asked for is bounded, and a long, detailed spec counts as evidence of bounded scope | required |
| ai-policy-ok        | The "contribution policy (CONTRIBUTING.md)" line under Repo facts                                                                                                                                                                          | The policy does not ban AI-generated or AI-assisted contributions. Conditions such as disclosure, personal understanding, testing, or human review still pass. "No statement on AI or contribution tooling" passes; silence is never unclear for this check                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | required  |
| fresh-issue         | The issue's "opened by ... on <date>" line, compared with the bundle's capture date                                                                                                                                                        | The issue was opened within the last 12 months of the capture date                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | preferred |

## Verdict rule

Accept if every required check passes; otherwise reject. Preferred checks never change the verdict; they only rank accepted issues. An `unclear` grade on any required check counts as fail, except ai-policy-ok, where the absence of any AI statement is a pass by definition and must be graded pass, not unclear.
